Our story

Simple tees,
made with obsession.

VELORA started with one question: why is it so hard to find a printed t-shirt that looks great and lasts? We spent months testing fabrics, weights and cuts until we landed on tees that feel premium, hold their shape and look great on their own — with prints that stay sharp wash after wash.

Ethically made locally. Cut and sewn in small batches by skilled tailors.

Colours that last. Reactive dyes that don't fade or bleed in the wash.

Fits for everyone. Regular, oversized and kids' cuts from S to XXL.
